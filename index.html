TikTokLike

A personal desktop tool for creating and publishing short vertical videos.

TikTokLike runs entirely on its owner's own computer. It cuts long videos into short vertical clips, writes a caption (title, description, hashtags) for each clip, and, after the owner connects their own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, uploads those clips to that same account through the TikTok Content Posting API, one after another, in order.

  • The owner reviews and can edit every caption before anything is sent.
  • Nothing is posted without an explicit confirmation by the owner in the app.
  • The owner chooses the visibility of every post.
  • The app does not run on a server and does not collect data from anyone.
